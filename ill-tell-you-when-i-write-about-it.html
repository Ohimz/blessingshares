I Will Tell You When I Write About It

Some stories cannot be written until they have been fully lived. In this thoughtful poetry collection, Blessing Musa reflects on faith, growth, purpose, waiting, healing, and trusting God's timing. ABOUT THE BOOK

A Story Still Being Written

'I Will Tell You When I Write About It' is a compilation of 18 beautiful poems about healing, living and dreaming.

This poetry book was written from the pain of the Covid-19 pandemic, groaning from a waiting season, and hope for lasting love.

In the pages of this poetry book, you will find notes on love, hurt, joy, dreams, and more amazingly forgiveness.
