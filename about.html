MEET BLESSING

Dreamer. Writer. Encourager.

Blessing Ofure Musa (née Okoro) is a dream actualization advocate, a certified project manager, and author of two books, ‘They Told Us To Dream’ – the first edition (2017) and ‘I Will Tell You When I Write About It’ (2023). She has an MBA from IÉSEG School of Management, France, and a bachelor’s degree in Economics from the prestigious Covenant University, Nigeria.

She is the founder of BlessingShares, a platform dedicated to inspiring people through writing, speaking, storytelling, mentorship, and biblical encouragement.

Her mantra remains, Be inspired! It is a state of the mind.

HER STORY

The Journey Into Writing

Blessing's writing journey began as a child. At the age of eleven, she wrote her first poem, "Growing Up." That single poem became the beginning of a lifelong passion for words.

Over the years, writing evolved from private journals into blogging, public speaking, books, mentorship, and inspiring people around the world.

Today, through BlessingShares, she continues encouraging readers to live intentionally, dream courageously, and trust God completely.
