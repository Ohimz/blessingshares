ABOUT THE BOOK

Book Description

Growing up, Blessing always believed dreams were gifts from God. But like many people, she encountered fear, uncertainty, and moments of self-doubt.

Through deeply personal experiences, original poetry, and biblical reflection, "They Told Us To Dream" invites readers to embrace their calling, overcome limiting beliefs, and pursue God's purpose with confidence.

This book has helped hundreds of readers across the world discover their purpose, and live their God-given dreams.
